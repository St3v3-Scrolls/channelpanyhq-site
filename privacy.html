Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This tool (channelpanyhq.com) connects to social media accounts via OAuth solely to schedule and publish content on behalf of the account owner. We collect and store only the OAuth access tokens and basic profile/account identifiers (username, channel ID) required for publishing and scheduling functionality. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Tokens can be revoked at any time by disconnecting the account in the connected platform's app settings.
